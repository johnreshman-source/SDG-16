Public Issue Reporting

Share a concern

You can submit a report without creating an account or providing contact details.

Before you submit

Choose a category and provide a meaningful description between 20 and 5,000 characters. Anonymous reporting is enabled by default. This form does not request your name, email, telephone number, home address, or geolocation.

If you are signed in and turn off anonymous reporting, this report is linked to your account so you can view its status in your account dashboard. Anonymous reports are not linked to your account and will not appear there.

A short-lived first-party session cookie supports CSRF protection and admin login. The application does not use analytics or identity-tracking cookies.

Technical anonymity cannot be absolutely guaranteed because hosting providers, network infrastructure, or server configuration may process connection metadata. Avoid including identifying details in the description if you do not want them stored.

This is an independent educational project, not an official complaint portal. The service is not monitored for emergencies and submitted reports are not automatically sent to police, courts, or government authorities.
