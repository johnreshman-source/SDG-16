Sustainable Development Goal 16

Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions

Peaceful communities, fair laws, respect for human rights, and accountable institutions help everyone live with dignity and take part in society.

Explore Awareness Know Your Rights Report an Issue
16

A foundation for progress

Trust makes communities stronger.

When people are safe, treated fairly, and heard, they can contribute to a more inclusive future.

  • Prevent violence and protect dignity
  • Make justice more accessible
  • Build transparent, accountable institutions

Why it matters

Fairness and trust support everyday life

Peace, justice, human rights, equality, and transparent institutions are connected: each helps people feel safe, respected, and able to seek a fair outcome.

01

Peace and safety

Non-violent communities give people room to learn, work, and resolve disagreements respectfully.

02

Justice and equality

Fair rules and meaningful access to justice help protect rights and ensure people can be heard.

03

Accountable institutions

Transparent decisions and clear accountability reduce misuse of power and strengthen public trust.

How reporting works

A simple, responsible way to share a concern

01

Describe the issue

Choose a category and provide a clear description. You do not need an account to submit a report.

02

Submit anonymously

Anonymous reporting is enabled by default. The form does not ask for your name or contact details.

03

Keep your reference

After the report is saved, you will receive a reference ID for your records. There is no public report lookup.

Independent educational project: This is not an official government complaint portal. Reports remain in this application's database and are not automatically sent to public authorities.
